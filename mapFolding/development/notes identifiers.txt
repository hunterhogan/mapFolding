nMinus1Factorial = factorial(n - 1)
nMinus1Factorial is a good identifier. Hypothetically, it could be next to
nPlus1Factorial = factorial(n + 1)
Because the word "less" is the same length as "plus", identifiers with "less" and "more" will stay
physically aligned in the code. For _only_ the alignment reason, I prefer "less" over "minus."
nLess1Factorial = factorial(n - 1)
nPlus1Factorial = factorial(n + 1)
nMinus1Factorial = factorial(n - 1)

Similarly, I prefer the pair "less than" and "more than" over "less than" and "greater than" _only_
because of the symmetric alignment of related identifiers in the code.

Luckily, in European languages other than English, the semiotics tend to be the equivalent of "n less
1" rather than "n minus 1" and "n [is] more than 2" rather than "n greater than 2". So, for many ESL
readers, nLess1 is more natural, for example, than nMinus1.
