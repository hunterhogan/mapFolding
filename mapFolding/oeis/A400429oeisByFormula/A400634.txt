%N Number of semi-meanders with n crossings and winding number n-12, for n >= 12.
%C A semi-meander is a semi-infinite, self-avoiding, directed curve that crosses a line one or more times.
%C The winding number of a semi-meander is the hypothetical minimum number of self-crossings that would be created if a ray were to start at the source of the semi-infinite directed curve and extend to infinity without crossing the line.
%C The maximum winding number of a semi-meander with n crossings is n-2. Thus a(n) counts semi-meanders whose winding number is 10 less than the maximum.
%C Equivalent to a closed curve (a loop) transversely crossing a ray, considered up to continuous deformation fixing the ray, with a winding number equal to the number of crossings that would be created if the fixed end of the ray were extended to form a line.
%C Analogized to a river (a semi-meander, meander, or loop) crossing a road (a ray, line, or line segment).
%C The sixth diagonal of A400429, reading diagonals from the maximal-winding edge.
